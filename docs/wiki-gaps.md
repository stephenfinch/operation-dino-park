## Facts from the sources (numbers)

**Build menu (8 categories):** Paths, Cleaner Station, Attractions, Environment, Amenities, Enclosures, Security, Demolish.
- Paths: Path $50 (limit 500), Trash Can $100, Bench $75, Fountain $1,500 (research, limit 10).
- Cleaner Station $3,000: hire cleaners who clean dirty paths. Cleaners ignore emergencies.
- Amenities: Park Entrance $5,000 (placed by the player from the Actions menu, more than one allowed; helipads, ATM),
  Kiosk $2,000 (main + side; max price $50; meals Sandwich $5, Dino Dog $10, Rex Burger $15 cost; sides chili / fries / salad),
  Rest Room $1,500 (max $10, queue), Souvenir Stand $2,000 (research; balloon / fun bag / dinosaur toy; free gift button / snow globe / keychain),
  Rest Area $1,500 (research; resting).
- Attractions: Viewing Vent $1,500 (must sit on a fence), Viewing Platform $3,000, Balloon Tour $4,000 (research, $50/ride, route poles, closed in storms),
  Safari Adventure $4,000 (research, track max 800 m), Viewing Dome $5,000 (research; access door outside, dome inside the enclosure).
- Attraction bias: Mainstream / Thrill (Thrill Window, Thrill Platform, Terror Dome) / Fun (Playpen, Playtime Platform, Dino Play Dome) /
  Authenticity (Wonder Window, Scenic Platform, Nature Dome). Dino experience = Thrills, Fun, Authenticity.
- Enclosures: Hatchery $6,000 (up to 5 eggs per batch, releases one at a time, crane lifts hay or a cow), Carnivore Feeder $1,500 (goat $50 / cow $80 per drop),
  Herbivore Feeder $1,500 (bale $20 / paleo bale $50 per drop), Auto Lure $2,000 (research; herb or carn gas; never / monthly / bimonthly / automatic; $100 per month),
  fences per 3-panel section: Low $300, Medium $750 (research), High $1,500 (research after Medium). Upgrading costs the difference.
- Security: Ranger Station $6,000 ($500 per month; helicopter: sedate, retire, adrenaline, vaccinate, muster, rescue off-path guests; fuel; won't auto-fly in storms),
  Security Camera $600 (limit 100; warns of breakouts / rampages), Avoidance Beacon $2,000 (limit 10), Sentry Turret $10,000 (limit 10; targets none / carn / herb / all),
  Visitor Shelter $2,000 (40 guests; stays shut if badly damaged).
- Environment: Modern Trees $90 per 3×3 brush, Paleo Trees $270 per 3×3, Lake $20–100, Mountain $200–500, Flatten $1–150.
  Modern plants can give herbivores Gastric Poisoning. Sauropods browse trees instead of feeders.

**Research (cost, time, category):** Medium Fence $300 1 mo; High Fence $5,000 2 mo; Visitor Shelter $2,500 1.5 mo; Security Camera $1,500 1 mo;
Sentry Turret $1,500 2 mo (needs Camera); Avoidance Beacon $2,000 1 mo; Binoculars $600 1 mo (platform range); Balloon $1,500 2 mo; Dome $1,250 2 mo;
Safari $2,000 2 mo (needs Balloon); Fountain $400 1 mo; Souvenir Stand $2,000 2 mo; Rest Area $1,500 1 mo; Weather Guard $500 1 mo (umbrellas + lightning protection);
Super Extractor $4,500 1 mo; Growth Booster $3,000 1.5 mo; Hatchery Immunization $5,000 2 mo; Lethal Gene $20,000 1.5 mo; Auto Lure $1,200 1.5 mo;
Gyro Stabilizer $2,000; Double Battery $1,500 1 mo; Retire $1,500 1 mo.
Vaccines (need one sick dinosaur first): Tick $1,000 1 mo; Bumblefoot $1,000 1 mo; Dino Belly $1,500 1.5 mo; Flu $1,750 1.5 mo; Gastric $2,000 2 mo; Rabies $3,000 2 mo.
Starters: path, bin, bench, hatchery, modern flora, low fence, feeders, vent, platform, entrance, cleaner station, ranger station, kiosk, rest room.

**Fossils:** teams cost free / $5k / $10k / $15k / $20k (max 5). Dig 240 s. Find: fossil 90% (sells $750), amber 10% ($1,000); ores gold $6,000 (90 in all),
opal $4,000, silver $2,500. Spot quality: Rich, Good, Average, Mediocre, Poor, Exhausted (18 uses Rich→Good, 15 per stage to Poor, 5 Poor→Exhausted).
Spot kinds: plains, river, mountain (2–4 teams). PC allows 5 dig sites (first free, more unlock with stars). Market opens 15 weeks after the park opens, max 20 items, 1–5 star quality.

**Guests:** Mainstream, Thrill Seekers (red/black clothes; carnivores hunting / eating / fighting), Fun Lovers (herbivores playing, socialising),
Dino Nerds (authenticity: same era / site / continent, friend species together; hate seeing feeders and hatcheries). Passport with complaints.
Deaths: carnivores, twisters, exposure off-path (hungry / tired / bladder). Off-path guests are rescued by the ranger helicopter.

**Dinosaurs:** friend species and favourite prey per species (see wiki). Brachiosaurus is never attacked (except by a rampaging Spinosaurus), rears up to scare carnivores.
Kentrosaurus panics near large herbivores. Only Velociraptor (small carn) and Triceratops (herb) rampage among non-large; Dilophosaurus, Ceratosaurus, Albertosaurus go comatose instead.
Raptors test Medium fences for weak points, never climb High. Only T. rex, Spinosaurus, Carcharodontosaurus break High fences. Death duels: T. rex vs Spinosaurus / Triceratops / Ankylosaurus / Stegosaurus.
Pachycephalosaurus and Torosaurus dominance fights choose a herd leader (crown icon). Dilophosaurus spits. Gallimimus is the only herbivore that can get rabies.
Fatal diseases: Tick, Rabies, Gastric only. Immunity is for life. "Immunize unit" / "Immunize species".

**Weather:** Sunny, Heatwave (thirst up, trees slowly die, guests want air-conditioning), Rain (lights on, jungle grows), Thunderstorm (lightning; balloon can be hit and crash), Twister (forecast two months ahead; follows a path).
**Actions menu:** Place Park Entrance, Research, Declare Emergency (guests go to shelters; no fatality fines), Go To Chopper, Go To Land Cruiser.
**Reports:** Performance (quarterly board meeting), Visitor (front page opinions / back page enjoyment + authenticity), Security (incidents + rating), Dinosaur (genome, population, lifespan), Finance, Structure, Entertainment (attraction profit, bias, score).
**Advisors (roles to mirror with our own names):** founder (stars, board meetings), finance (board, finance), control room (facilities, weather), warden (security), geneticist (research), paleontologist (fossils), vet (welfare), PR (visitors, structures, entertainment, new-species PA announcements), assistant (construction).
**Island generator:** circle or square, size, forest, rivers, mountains. Hammond introduces the job.
**Site B:** unlimited money; only hatcheries, lures, feeders and terraforming; pick 5 dig sites; those species start at 100% DNA.
**Missions (10):** Safari (photo points), Ranger Attack (retire carnivores, limited ammo, timer), Dino Muster (herd herbivores into a muster area with the helicopter), Ranger Rescue (retire rampagers, rescue a VIP, drive a jeep to fetch a rifle).
**Exercises (9):** 4 tutorials (controls; fossils & DNA; visitor needs; security) + 5 scenarios (open-range herbivores; classic island; clean up Site B; carnivore island; storms).
